Breaking the Hidden Myths

SEO is often sold as a "secret formula," but the **Hidden Truth** is simpler: Google is an answer engine. Its only goal is to provide the most reliable, fastest, and most authoritative answer to a user's question. If your site isn't answering the question better than others, no amount of "tricks" will keep you at the top.
