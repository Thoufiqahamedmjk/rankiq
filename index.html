The 4-Phase Authority Model

Phase 1: Technical Infrastructure

Crawlability, indexing, speed optimization, mobile responsiveness, internal linking structure, and canonical clarity. Without technical strength, authority collapses.

Phase 2: Semantic Architecture

Topic clusters, structured hierarchy, internal contextual linking, and deep content coverage. Authority is built through completeness.

Phase 3: Authority Signals

Backlinks, brand mentions, engagement signals, transparency, and consistent publishing. Trust must be earned externally.

Phase 4: Conversion Alignment

Clear call-to-action, logical user journey, friction reduction, and trust reinforcement. Traffic without conversion is noise.
